Crochetzies — Size Guide
========================
Crochetzies offers three standard sizes for all items:

Small:
  Approximately palm-sized. Great for keychains, bag charms, desk decorations, and small gifts.
  Price range: Rs. 500 to Rs. 800

Medium:
  Approximately the size of a small stuffed toy. Great for gifts, display, and cuddly companions.
  Price range: Rs. 900 to Rs. 1400

Large:
  A bigger, more impressive plushie. Great for statement gifts and children.
  Price range: Rs. 1500 to Rs. 2500

All prices are per item and depend on complexity and extras. Prices multiply by quantity.
