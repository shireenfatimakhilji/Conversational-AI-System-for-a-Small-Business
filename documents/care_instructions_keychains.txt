Crochetzies — Care Instructions for Keychains
===============================================
Crochet keychains are durable but benefit from gentle care:

  - Avoid soaking in water for extended periods
  - If dirty, spot clean with a damp cloth and mild soap
  - Allow to air dry completely before using again
  - Avoid exposing to excessive heat (e.g. leaving in a hot car)
  - The metal keychain ring is sturdy but avoid bending it repeatedly

Keychains are decorative items and may show wear over time with heavy daily use, which is normal for handmade goods.
