Crochetzies — Crochet Bunny
============================
The crochet bunny is sweet and soft with long floppy ears and a tiny pom-pom tail. Perfect for Easter, baby showers, or as a cuddly companion.

Available sizes: Small, Medium, Large
Customization options:
  - Any color (white, grey, pink, lavender, brown)
  - Bow or ribbon on ear
  - Sleepy or wide-awake expression
  - Outfit (tiny dress or vest)
  - Keychain version available in Small

Price:
  Small  : Rs. 500 – 800
  Medium : Rs. 900 – 1400
  Large  : Rs. 1500 – 2500
