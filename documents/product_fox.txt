Crochetzies — Crochet Fox
==========================
The crochet fox features pointy ears, a bushy tail with a white tip, and a charming face. A great gift for nature lovers.

Available sizes: Small, Medium, Large
Customization options:
  - Classic orange and white, or custom colors (arctic white, grey, purple fantasy)
  - Striped or plain tail
  - Tiny flower crown or bow
  - Keychain version available in Small

Price:
  Small  : Rs. 500 – 800
  Medium : Rs. 900 – 1400
  Large  : Rs. 1500 – 2500
