Crochetzies — Birthday Gift Ideas
====================================
A handmade crochet plushie is a heartfelt and unique birthday gift. Popular birthday choices:

  - A plushie of the recipient's favorite animal
  - A mini doll resembling the birthday person
  - A unicorn or dragon for fantasy lovers
  - A food plushie (avocado, strawberry) for a fun quirky gift
  - Keychains for teens and adults

Add a name tag or embroidered initial to make it extra personal. All items come gift-wrapped at no extra charge.
