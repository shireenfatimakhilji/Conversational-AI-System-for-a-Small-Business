Crochetzies — Care Instructions for Baby-Safe Items
=====================================================
If you ordered an item for a baby or toddler, the following applies:

  - All items made for babies use embroidered eyes and details (no plastic attachments)
  - Still supervise young children during play
  - Hand wash only in cold water with baby-safe detergent
  - Rinse very thoroughly to remove all detergent
  - Air dry completely before giving to the child
  - Inspect regularly for any loose threads and trim if needed

Baby-safe items are made with extra care to ensure there are no small parts that could pose a choking hazard.
