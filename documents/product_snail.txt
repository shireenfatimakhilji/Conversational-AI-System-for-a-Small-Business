Crochetzies — Crochet Snail
============================
Our crochet snail has a coiled shell and a stretchy little body. Slow and sweet.

Available sizes: Small, Medium, Large
Customization options:
  - Any shell color (brown, pink, blue, rainbow spiral)
  - Body in contrasting color
  - Tiny flower or leaf accessory
  - Keychain version available in Small

Price:

  Medium : Rs. 900 – 1400
  Large  : Rs. 1500 – 2500
