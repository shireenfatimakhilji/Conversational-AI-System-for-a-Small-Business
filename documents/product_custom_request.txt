Crochetzies — Custom and One-of-a-Kind Requests
=================================================
At Crochetzies, we love creative challenges. If you have something in mind that is not in our standard product list, you can still request it.

What counts as a custom request:
  - An animal or creature we have not listed (e.g. a capybara, manta ray, axolotl variant, platypus)
  - A food or object plushie (e.g. a crochet pizza slice, sushi, boba tea cup, cloud)
  - A character you designed yourself
  - A scene or diorama (e.g. a tiny crochet garden, a miniature room)
  - A very specific color combination or pattern not in our standard range
  - A large or unusually complex item

How to request:
  Describe what you want as clearly as possible — what it looks like, what colors, what size, and any special details. You can also send a reference image on WhatsApp.

Pricing for custom requests:
  Custom items are priced based on complexity and time required. We will give you a quote after reviewing your request. Pricing still roughly follows our size tiers (Small, Medium, Large) but may be higher for very complex or large items.

One thing we do not do:
  We do not replicate licensed or copyrighted characters (cartoon characters, brand mascots, etc.) due to copyright reasons. We are happy to make something inspired by a theme or color scheme, but not an exact copy of a trademarked character.
