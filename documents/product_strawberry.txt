Crochetzies — Crochet Strawberry
==================================
The crochet strawberry is a sweet fruit plushie with seed detail and a leafy green top. Cheerful and bright.

Available sizes: Small, Medium, Large
Customization options:
  - Red (classic) or fantasy colors (purple, blue)
  - Happy or sleepy face
  - Keychain version available in Small

Price:
  Small  : Rs. 500 – 800
  Medium : Rs. 900 – 1400
  Large  : Rs. 1500 – 2500
