Crochetzies — General Care Instructions
========================================
To keep your Crochetzies item looking its best:

Washing:
  - Hand wash gently in cold or lukewarm water
  - Use a mild detergent
  - Do not scrub or wring
  - Rinse thoroughly

Drying:
  - Gently squeeze out excess water
  - Lay flat to dry or hang in shade
  - Do not tumble dry or expose to direct sunlight for long periods
  - Do not use a dryer as it may damage the yarn or shape

Storage:
  - Store in a dry, cool place away from direct sunlight
  - Avoid storing in damp or humid areas
  - Keep away from sharp objects that could snag the yarn

With proper care, your handmade crochet item will last for many years.
