Crochetzies — Payment & Delivery Policy
========================================
Payment Method:
  Crochetzies accepts Cash on Delivery (COD) only. No advance payment is required. You pay when your order arrives at your door.

Delivery Time:
  All orders are delivered within 5 to 7 business days after the order is confirmed. Business days exclude weekends and public holidays.

Delivery Coverage:
  We deliver across Pakistan. Delivery charges may vary by city and will be communicated at the time of order confirmation.

Order Confirmation:
  After placing your order via the chatbot or WhatsApp, our team will contact you to confirm the details before production begins.
