Crochetzies — Crochet Ghost
============================
Our crochet ghost is more cute than spooky — round, white, with a tiny surprised or happy face.

Available sizes: Small, Medium, Large
Customization options:
  - White (classic) or pastel colors (pink, blue, mint)
  - Witch hat for Halloween version
  - Tiny pumpkin companion add-on
  - Keychain version available in Small

Price:
  Small  : Rs. 500 – 800
  Medium : Rs. 900 – 1400
  Large  : Rs. 1500 – 2500
