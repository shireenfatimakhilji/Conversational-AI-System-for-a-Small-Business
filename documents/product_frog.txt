Crochetzies — Crochet Frog
===========================
Our crochet frog is round, squishy-looking, and irresistibly cute with big bulgy eyes and tiny webbed feet.

Available sizes: Small, Medium, Large
Customization options:
  - Classic green, or fantasy colors (blue, purple, yellow, tie-dye)
  - Little crown (frog prince version)
  - Flower or lily pad accessory
  - Keychain version available in Small

Price:
  Small  : Rs. 500 – 800
  Medium : Rs. 900 – 1400
  Large  : Rs. 1500 – 2500
