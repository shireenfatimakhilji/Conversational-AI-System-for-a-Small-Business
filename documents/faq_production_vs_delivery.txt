FAQ: What is the difference between production time and delivery time?
=======================================================================
Great question — these are two separate things.

Production time:
  This is the time it takes for us to actually handcraft your item after the order is confirmed. Because everything is made by hand and to order, production typically takes 3 to 5 business days depending on the complexity of the item and how many orders we have at that time.

Delivery time:
  This is the time it takes for your package to travel from us to you after it has been dispatched. Delivery within the same city is usually 1 to 2 business days. Delivery to other cities in Pakistan may take 2 to 3 business days.

Total time:
  The overall 5 to 7 business day estimate we give covers both production and delivery combined.

If you need your order by a specific date (for example, for Eid or a birthday), please let us know when placing your order and we will tell you honestly whether it is possible.
