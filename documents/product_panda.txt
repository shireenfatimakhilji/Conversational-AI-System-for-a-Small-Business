Crochetzies — Crochet Panda
============================
Our crochet panda has the iconic black-and-white pattern with round ears and a chubby body.

Available sizes: Small, Medium, Large
Customization options:
  - Classic black and white, or color panda (pink, blue, red)
  - Bamboo accessory it holds
  - Sitting or lying down pose
  - Keychain version available in Small

Price:
  Small  : Rs. 500 – 800
  Medium : Rs. 900 – 1400
  Large  : Rs. 1500 – 2500
