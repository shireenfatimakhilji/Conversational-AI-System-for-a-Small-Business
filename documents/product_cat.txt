Crochetzies — Crochet Cat
==========================
The crochet cat is one of our most popular items. It comes in a sitting or standing pose with a sweet face, round ears, and a curled tail.

Available sizes: Small, Medium, Large
Customization options:
  - Any color or pattern (tabby stripes, calico patches, solid colors)
  - Collar with or without a small bow or bell
  - Sleepy, happy, or grumpy expression
  - Matching pair of cats (e.g. mother and kitten)
  - Keychain version available in Small

Popular color choices: white, grey, orange, black, cream, brown, ginger

Price:

  Medium : Rs. 900 – 1400
  Large  : Rs. 1500 – 2500
