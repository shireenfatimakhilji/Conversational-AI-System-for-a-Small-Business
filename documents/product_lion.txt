Crochetzies — Crochet Lion
===========================
The crochet lion has a magnificent fluffy mane and a proud stance. A bold and impressive gift.

Available sizes: Small, Medium, Large
Customization options:

  - Crown accessory (king of the jungle version)
  - Cub version without a mane

Price:

  Medium : Rs. 900 – 1400
