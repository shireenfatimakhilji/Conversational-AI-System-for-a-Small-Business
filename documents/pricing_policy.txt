Crochetzies — Pricing Policy
=============================
Prices at Crochetzies depend on the size of the item:

  Small  : Rs. 500 to Rs. 800
  Medium : Rs. 900 to Rs. 1400
  Large  : Rs. 1500 to Rs. 2500

The range within each size depends on:
  - Number of colors used
  - Complexity of the design
  - Number of extras or add-ons requested
  - Fine detail work (embroidery, accessories, etc.)

For multiple items, the subtotal is calculated by multiplying the unit price range by the quantity.

Example:
  2 Medium cats = Rs. 1800 to Rs. 2800

All payments are Cash on Delivery. No hidden fees.
