FAQ: What yarn colors are available?
======================================
We work with a wide range of yarn colors. Here is a general guide to what we typically stock:

Neutrals:
  White, off-white, cream, beige, light grey, dark grey, charcoal, black, brown, tan, caramel

Pastels:
  Baby pink, blush, lavender, mint green, sky blue, peach, lilac, pale yellow, powder blue

Brights / Vivid:
  Red, hot pink, magenta, coral, orange, sunshine yellow, lime green, bright green, royal blue, cobalt, purple, teal, turquoise

Earthy tones:
  Olive green, mustard yellow, terracotta, rust, sage green, dusty rose, warm brown

Specialty:
  Gold (metallic-effect), silver (metallic-effect), rainbow variegated yarn

If you have a very specific shade in mind (for example, a particular pastel or a custom mix), describe it to us and we will do our best to match it. We regularly update our yarn stock, so availability of specific shades may vary.

If a color you want is not in stock, we will let you know at the time of order confirmation and suggest the closest available alternative.
