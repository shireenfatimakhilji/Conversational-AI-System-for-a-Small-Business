Crochetzies — Customization Guide
==================================
Every item at Crochetzies is fully customizable. Here is what you can personalize:

Colors:
  You can choose any color or combination of colors for your item. We work with a wide range of yarn colors including pastels, neons, neutrals, and more. If you have a specific shade in mind, describe it and we will do our best to match it.

Sizes:
  All items come in three sizes:
    - Small
    - Medium
    - Large
  Size refers to the overall dimensions of the finished item. If you need a specific measurement, contact us and we can discuss.

Extras / Add-ons:
  You can request extras such as:
    - Bow ties or ribbons
    - Hats or accessories
    - Name tags or initials
    - Scarves
    - Wings (for birds, fairies, dragons, etc.)
    - Custom facial expressions (happy, sleepy, etc.)
    - Keychains attached to small items
    - Matching outfits for pairs
  If you want something not listed here, just ask — we love creative challenges.

Quantity:
  You can order as many pieces as you want. Bulk orders for events like weddings or corporate gifts are welcome.
