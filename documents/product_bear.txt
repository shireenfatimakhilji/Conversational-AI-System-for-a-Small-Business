Crochetzies — Crochet Bear
===========================
Our crochet bear is a classic plushie with round ears, a plump belly, and a gentle face. Available as a teddy bear or panda variant.

Available sizes: Small, Medium, Large
Customization options:
  - Any color (brown, cream, white, black, pink)
  - Panda black-and-white pattern
  - Bow tie, scarf, or hat
  - Heart on chest or embroidered name
  - Keychain version available in Small

Price:
  Small  : Rs. 500 – 800
  Medium : Rs. 900 – 1400
  Large  : Rs. 1500 – 2500
