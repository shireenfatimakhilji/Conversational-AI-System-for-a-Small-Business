Crochetzies — Crochet Mini Doll
=================================
Our crochet mini dolls are small humanoid figures with yarn hair, tiny outfits, and customizable features. Popular as personalized gifts.

Available sizes: Small, Medium, Large
Customization options:
  - Skin tone (wide range available)
  - Hair color and style (bun, ponytail, loose, curly, hijab)
  - Outfit color and style (dress, traditional shalwar kameez, casual)
  - Custom accessory (miniature book, flower, sports item)
  - Couple dolls or family sets

  Medium : Rs. 900 – 1400
  Large  : Rs. 1500 – 2500
