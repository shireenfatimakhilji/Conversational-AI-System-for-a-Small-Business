Crochetzies — Keychains Overview
==================================
Almost any Small-size plushie can be made into a keychain by attaching a metal ring and chain. Popular keychain choices include:

  - Mini animals (cat, dog, frog, bunny, bear, axolotl)
  - Food items (strawberry, avocado, mushroom, donut)
  - Fantasy items (ghost, star, heart, moon)
  - Mini dolls

Keychains are priced in the Small range: Rs. 500 – 800 each.

They make excellent:
  - Bag charms
  - Car key accessories
  - School bag decorations
  - Party favors
  - Small gifts
