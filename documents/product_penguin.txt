Crochetzies — Crochet Penguin
================================
The crochet penguin has a round body, little flippers, and an endearing waddling pose. Great for winter themes and gifting.

Available sizes: Small, Medium, Large
Customization options:
  - Classic black and white, or fantasy colors (blue, purple, pink)
  - Tiny scarf or bow tie
  - Graduation cap version for a congratulations gift
  - Keychain version available in Small

Price:
  Small  : Rs. 500 – 800
  Medium : Rs. 900 – 1400
  Large  : Rs. 1500 – 2500
