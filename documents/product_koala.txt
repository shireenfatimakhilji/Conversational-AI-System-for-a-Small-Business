Crochetzies — Crochet Koala
============================
The crochet koala has large rounded ears, a big soft nose, and a sleepy expression.

Available sizes: Small, Medium, Large
Customization options:
  - Grey, brown, or custom color
  - Eucalyptus leaf or baby koala on back
  - Keychain version available in Small

Price:
  Small  : Rs. 500 – 800
  Medium : Rs. 900 – 1400
  Large  : Rs. 1500 – 2500
