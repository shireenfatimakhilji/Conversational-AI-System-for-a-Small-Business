FAQ: What if I receive the wrong item or there is a problem?
==============================================================
If your order arrives and something is not right — wrong color, wrong size, damaged during delivery, or missing an extra you requested — please contact us immediately.

How to report an issue:
  Email    : crochetzies@gmail.com
  WhatsApp : +92-300-0000000 (fastest response)

When contacting us, please share:
  - Your name and order details
  - A photo of the item you received
  - A description of what the problem is

We take every complaint seriously. Since all items are handmade to order, small natural variations in texture or shade are normal and not considered defects. However, if the item is clearly wrong or damaged, we will work with you to make it right.

Please note: We do not accept returns on custom orders, but we will always try to resolve genuine issues fairly.
