Crochetzies — Crochet Duck
===========================
The crochet duck is cheerful and round with a bright beak and tiny wings. A perfect baby shower or Eid gift.

Available sizes: Small, Medium, Large
Customization options:
  - Classic yellow, or any color (pink, blue, lavender)
  - Sailor hat or bow
  - Mama duck and duckling pair
