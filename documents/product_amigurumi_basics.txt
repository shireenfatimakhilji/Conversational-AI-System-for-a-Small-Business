Crochetzies — Amigurumi
========================
Amigurumi is the Japanese art of knitting or crocheting small, stuffed yarn creatures. All Crochetzies plushies are made in the amigurumi style.

Key characteristics:
  - Round, compact shapes
  - Oversized heads relative to body (cute proportions)
  - Safety eyes or embroidered eyes
  - Polyester fiberfill stuffing
  - Individually crocheted limbs attached to the body

Amigurumi items can represent animals, food, fantasy creatures, objects, or characters. Any idea can be turned into an amigurumi at Crochetzies — just ask!
