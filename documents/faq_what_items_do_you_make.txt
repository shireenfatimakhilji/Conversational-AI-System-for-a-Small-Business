FAQ: What items does Crochetzies make?
=======================================
Crochetzies makes a wide variety of handmade crochet items, including:

  - Stuffed animals (cats, dogs, bunnies, bears, foxes, elephants, and many more)
  - Fantasy creatures (dragons, unicorns, dinosaurs, axolotls)
  - Sea creatures (sharks, octopuses, jellyfish, whales, crabs, turtles)
  - Insects and bugs (bees, butterflies, ladybugs, caterpillars)
  - Food plushies (strawberries, mushrooms, avocados)
  - Decorative items (stars, rainbows, suns, ghosts)
  - Mini dolls and amigurumi characters
  - Keychains and bag charms
  - Bookmarks and hair accessories

If you have something specific in mind that is not listed, just ask — we love custom ideas!
