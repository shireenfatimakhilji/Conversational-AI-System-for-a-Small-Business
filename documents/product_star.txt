Crochetzies — Crochet Star

Our crochet star plushie is a five-pointed pillow-style star with a smiling face. Great for nursery decor and gifts.

Available sizes: Small, Medium, Large
Customization options:

  - Face or plain (no face)
  - Hanging loop for nursery mobile
  - Keychain version available in Small
