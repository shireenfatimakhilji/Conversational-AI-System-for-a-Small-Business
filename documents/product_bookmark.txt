Crochetzies — Crochet Bookmarks
=================================
Crochet bookmarks are flat or lightly stuffed decorative bookmarks with a character on top that peeks out of the book.

Popular designs:
  - Animal heads (cat, dog, fox, owl)
  - Food characters (strawberry, mushroom)
  - Fantasy characters (ghost, star)

Customization options:
  - Any color
  - Any character design
