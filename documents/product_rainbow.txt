Crochetzies — Crochet Rainbow
================================
The crochet rainbow is an arc-shaped plushie with colorful stripes and little cloud ends. A cheerful home decor item.

Available sizes: Small, Medium, Large
Customization options:
  - Classic rainbow colors or custom palette (pastels, neons)
  - Cloud in white or custom color
  - Hanging loop for display

Price:
  Small  : Rs. 500 – 800
  Medium : Rs. 900 – 1400
  Large  : Rs. 1500 – 2500
