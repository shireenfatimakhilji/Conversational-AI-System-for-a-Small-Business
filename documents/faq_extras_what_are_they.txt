FAQ: What kinds of extras can I add?
======================================
Extras are optional add-ons that make your item more special. Examples include:

  - Bow ties or ribbons
  - Hats (witch hat, graduation cap, birthday hat, tiny knit hat)
  - Scarves
  - Name tags or embroidered initials
  - Wings (for dragons, butterflies, fairies, unicorns)
  - Crowns or flower crowns
  - Accessories the character holds (a tiny heart, flower, leaf, book, etc.)
  - Outfits (small dress, vest, shalwar kameez)

There may be a small additional charge for complex extras. We will let you know at order confirmation.
