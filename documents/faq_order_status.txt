FAQ: How do I check on my order status?
=========================================
After placing your order, our team will contact you on WhatsApp or via email to confirm your order details before production begins.

Once production starts, here is the typical timeline:
  - Day 0     : Order confirmed, production begins
  - Day 1–5   : Item is being handcrafted
  - Day 5–7   : Item is gift-wrapped and dispatched for delivery
  - Day 5–7   : Delivery to your address

To check on your order at any time, contact us directly:
  WhatsApp : +92-300-0000000
  Email    : crochetzies@gmail.com

Please have your name and order details ready when you reach out. We will give you an update as quickly as possible.

We do not currently have an automated order tracking system. All updates are communicated personally by our team.
