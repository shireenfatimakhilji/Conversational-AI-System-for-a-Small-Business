Crochetzies — Crochet Ladybug
================================
The crochet ladybug is round and cheerful with a spotted red shell and little black legs.

Available sizes: Small, Medium, Large
Customization options:
  - Classic red with black spots, or custom colors
  - Number of spots
  - Keychain version available in Small

Price:
  Small  : Rs. 500 – 800
  Medium : Rs. 900 – 1400
  Large  : Rs. 1500 – 2500
