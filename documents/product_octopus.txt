Crochetzies — Crochet Octopus
================================
The crochet octopus has eight curly tentacles and a round head with an expressive face. A quirky and loveable choice.

Available sizes: Small, Medium, Large
Customization options:
  - Any color (orange, purple, pink, blue, red)
  - Reversible mood (happy/sad flip design)
  - Crown or bow accessory
  - Keychain version available in Small

Price:
  Small  : Rs. 500 – 800
  Medium : Rs. 900 – 1400
  Large  : Rs. 1500 – 2500
