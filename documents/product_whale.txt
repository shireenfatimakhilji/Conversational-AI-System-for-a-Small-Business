Crochetzies — Crochet Whale
============================
Our crochet whale is round and soft with a little spout on top and a smiling face.

Available sizes: Small, Medium, Large
Customization options:

  - Water spout in white or contrasting color
  - Keychain version available in Small

Price:

  Medium : Rs. 900 – 1400
