Crochetzies — Crochet Elephant (Variants)
==========================================
Beyond the standard elephant, Crochetzies also offers:
  - Baby elephant (smaller, rounder, with big wide eyes)
  - Elephant with a blanket or saddle
  - Elephant family set (mama and calf)
  - Elephant with a flower in its trunk

All variants are customizable in color and size. Price is the same as standard:
  Small  : Rs. 500 – 800
  Medium : Rs. 900 – 1400
  Large  : Rs. 1500 – 2500
