Crochetzies — Crochet Caterpillar
===================================
The crochet caterpillar is made of a chain of colorful round segments with tiny antennae and a happy face.

Available sizes: Small, Medium, Large (more or fewer segments)
Customization options:
  - Rainbow segments or custom color pattern
  - Number of segments
  - Tiny flower or hat on head

Price:
  Small  : Rs. 500 – 800
  Medium : Rs. 900 – 1400
  Large  : Rs. 1500 – 2500
