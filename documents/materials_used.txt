Crochetzies — Materials
=======================
All Crochetzies items are made using high-quality soft yarn. The filling used inside plushies and stuffed animals is hypoallergenic polyester fiberfill, which is soft, safe, and washable.

Eyes on stuffed animals are either:
  - Safety eyes (plastic, securely fastened — safe for older children and adults)
  - Embroidered eyes (fully stitched — recommended for babies and toddlers)

If you are ordering for a baby or toddler, please let us know and we will use embroidered details instead of any small attachments.

Yarn colors are vibrant and fade-resistant. All items are handcrafted with love and attention to detail.
